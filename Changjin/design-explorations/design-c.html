Inside one FDA equivalence group, price spans more than two orders of magnitude.

Every dot is one surveyed product. Every row is one group FDA rates therapeutically equivalent, so each row is a set of products a pharmacist may swap between. The axis is logarithmic and shared, and rows are ordered by how far apart the cheapest and dearest sit.

Built 2026-08-31 from Orange Book products.txt and the CMS NADAC survey covering 2025-01-01 to 2026-08-26. Every figure is a pharmacy acquisition cost per dispensing unit, which is neither a copay nor a cash price.
